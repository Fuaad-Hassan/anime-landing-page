Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan grabbed me from the very first episode. The action, mysteries, and big surprises kept me glued to the screen, and the story of the Titans’ origin was what really made the series so engaging for me.

Sometimes I wished a few things had gone differently, but the excitement and adrenaline throughout made the whole ride really memorable. Every reveal and twist kept me wanting to see what would happen next.


Action

Fate Stay Night

The animation was the first thing that really got my attention. It looked incredibly good, which is why I started watching, then it pulled me into a world where old heroes come back to fight. Following Shirou's journey in the Holy Grail War kept me highly interested and curious what was next.

The big battles, the unique characters, and how the story progressed made it very entertaining. I truly enjoyed this anime the whole way through.


Fantasy

Made in Abyss

The concept of the Abyss this huge mysterious hole full of secrets was the first thing that really got my attention. Watching Riko and Reg go deeper and deeper made me feel excited but also very nervous. Each new layer of the Abyss brings unique danger and mystery, which kept me completely focused.

Their difficult journey and strong friendship made the whole adventure feel so real. I can’t wait to see what they will find as they keep exploring the very bottom of the Abyss.


Adventure

Naruto

If I had to choose one favorite anime from my younger years, it would definitely be Naruto. As kids, we connected with Naruto’s never-give-up attitude and enjoyed the strong theme of friendship that ran through the story.

Rewatching it as an adult gave me a new perspective. I saw much deeper things. You realize how complex the characters and their backstories are and how everything in the story connects together. It was fun to rewatch and the series still feels just as good today.


Shounen

Vinland Saga

For any series there is always something that captures your interest right away and with Vinland Saga it was the historical setting the world of the Vikings. It appealed to me first because of this setting.

The visual execution and combat sequences were magnificent. Following Thorfinn from a furious boy blinded by vengeance to a man seeking peace shows a deep character change. It is a compelling story about what a true warrior really means.


Historical

Violet Evergarden

What struck me first was the animation absolutely stunning. Violet’s work as an Auto Memory Doll sounds simple, but for someone raised only for war, understanding emotions was a huge struggle. How could she write feelings she never learned to feel?

The stories are emotional without being overwhelming. Watching Violet grow through each person she met was beautiful new faces, new emotions, all brought to life by incredible animation. It’s a show that reaches your heart before your mind. A true masterpiece.
